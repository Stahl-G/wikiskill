# Verify the Exact Mathematical Claim

Before choosing an option, identify the theorem or calculation being tested and preserve every hypothesis.

- Translate each option into explicit logical form. Track who chooses each object, whether a choice is fixed or depends on another object, and whether the claim is for one object, infinitely many, arbitrarily large objects, or all sufficiently large parameters.
- For “strongest” questions, compare implication strength. Reject options that add an unsupported moreover-clause, equivalence, uniformity, global conclusion, or extra preservation property. Select the strongest statement actually forced by the hypotheses.
- Stress-test every added clause adversarially before ranking options. For an endpoint, exception, parity or exact-count claim, componentwise strengthening, universal quantifier, or moreover-clause, test the smallest permitted parameter or a degenerate admissible case. A failure of one added clause rejects the whole option; survival means the clause must still be verified, not dismissed merely because it is stronger.
- Keep every qualifier attached to the claim: domain, endpoint, local-versus-global scope, regularity, exceptional alternative, exclusion, and side condition. Do not broaden a classification or silently discard an exception.
- For formulas and asymptotics, compare term by term. Check exponents, signs, factors, normalization, and defining constants by substitution or scaling. When specializing, satisfy every stated relation; distinguish O from o and do not accept a sharper error term without justification.
- For bounds, inspect the exact quantifier range and logarithmic or iterated-exponential factors. A nearby exponent or omitted factor changes the statement.
- For equality or classification claims, verify both directions and all equality conditions; similarity of the surrounding theorem is not evidence that a stronger conclusion follows.
- Eliminate an option as soon as one conjunct, quantifier, qualifier, or numerical detail is unsupported. Do not use option position, length, or wording style as evidence.

Only after this audit, choose the label whose entire statement is justified.
